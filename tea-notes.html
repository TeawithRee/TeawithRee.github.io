Tea Notes · private coaching with Rehana Rutti

A quieter way to begin your next chapter

Thoughtful, practical coaching for people who are ready to stop circling the change and start making room for it.

Begin with a discovery application Explore the programmes

Tea Notes brings the reflective spirit of Tea with Ree into a confidential coaching relationship. We make space for what is true, choose a direction that feels meaningful, and turn insight into small, repeatable actions.

A personal, non-clinical coaching space. Tea Notes is not therapy, medical care, psychiatric care, crisis support or a substitute for professional treatment. If you are in immediate danger or need urgent mental-health support, contact local emergency services or a qualified crisis service in your country.
The programme pathways
Reset · four weeks

A clear place to start

For a focused season when you need to hear yourself think, name what is changing and create a grounded next step.

  • Four weekly private coaching sessions
  • A simple focus and action rhythm between calls
  • A closing reflection and next-step map
Apply for Reset
Renewal · twelve weeks

Make the change livable

For the deeper work of changing patterns, rebuilding trust in yourself and giving a new direction enough time to take root.

  • Fortnightly coaching with a midpoint reset
  • Practical experiments between sessions
  • A personal renewal review and continuation plan
Apply for Renewal
Becoming · twenty-four weeks

Live into what is next

An invitation-only, longer relationship for a significant transition that calls for steadiness, perspective and room to evolve.

  • Weekly or weekly-lite cadence agreed together
  • Milestones that keep the middle from going quiet
  • A spacious review for the chapter ahead
Request a conversation

Good coaching begins with a good fit.

The application is a conversation starter, not an automatic acceptance or a diagnosis. Rehana reviews each application manually and may recommend a different level of support, ask for a short introductory call, or suggest a suitably qualified professional where coaching is not the right setting.

Submitting an application does not create a coaching agreement, client relationship or guarantee acceptance. If a programme is offered, the coaching agreement, terms and privacy information will be provided before paid coaching begins.

For your privacy, please do not include medical records, diagnoses, medication details, trauma histories, financial information or emergency disclosures in this form.

A possible companion, later.

Tea Note Sessions is an optional companion we may discuss later for private journaling, guided reflections and thoughtful prompts between sessions. It is not required to enquire about or take part in Tea Notes, and it is not therapy, medical care or a replacement for live coaching.

The companion will only be introduced when its purpose, privacy arrangements and support boundaries are ready. For now, the main experience is the coaching relationship with Rehana.
