Tea Notes

Some things are easier to understand when you say them out loud.

A private coaching conversation for the things you’re thinking about, sitting with, or ready to change. Bring the question.

We’ll start there.

Reset

A reset can be as simple as stopping for a moment and being honest about how things are going. We can look at what is taking up too much space, what you have been carrying and what might help you feel a little clearer.

Renew

Renewal is about finding your way back to the things that give you energy. That might mean making a change, returning to something you have missed, or giving yourself permission to want something different.

Becoming

Becoming is about the person you are growing into. You may not have the whole picture yet. That is perfectly all right. We can explore the questions, the possibilities and the next small step together.

Begin with a conversation

Sometimes the best place to begin is simply to talk. Bring the question, the change you are considering, or the thing you have not quite found the words for yet.
