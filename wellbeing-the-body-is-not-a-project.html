The Body Is Not a Project

There are days when movement feels joyful and days when it feels like one more demand. I have learned that both can be true without turning the body into a problem to solve.

Wellbeing language can make us feel as if we are always behind. Better sleep. Better food. More strength. A smaller waist. A longer run. There is always another version of ourselves waiting at the end of the next programme.

The body is a home you live in, not a project you are never allowed to finish.

I like the feeling of being strong enough to carry my own life. I also like rest, celebration and the occasional day when the plan changes. Health is not a punishment for having a body. It is a relationship with one.

Move because it helps you feel more present. Rest because recovery is part of the work. Let the body be a home you live in, not a project you are never allowed to finish.
