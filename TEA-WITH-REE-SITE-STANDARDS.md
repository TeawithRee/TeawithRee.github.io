# Tea with Ree site standards and operating guide

**Prepared for:** Rehana Rutti  
**Site:** [teawithree.com](https://teawithree.com/)  
**Date:** 13 September 2026

## Executive summary

Tea with Ree works best as a quiet editorial site with a coaching business beneath the writing rather than a coaching sales site dressed as a magazine. The strongest reference is Tea Notes. Its composition depends on generous whitespace, a controlled reading measure, warm restraint and a clear distinction between literary display type and functional interface type.

The current system uses Cormorant Garamond for the brand, headings and article titles. It uses Manrope for body copy, labels, navigation and controls. This pairing should remain stable. The homepage may remain more expressive, but About, Tea Notes, Ecology, Inner Work, Modern Tools and Wellbeing should share the same underlying spacing, margin and reading rhythm.

This document is the working reference for writing, editing, image handling, SEO, performance, publishing and final checking. It does not require every existing article to be rewritten. A short article may remain short. The rules below govern new work and audits unless Rehana gives a specific exception.

## The editorial promise

Every piece should work as an essay before it works as part of a business. A reader should be able to encounter the page without knowing anything about coaching and still feel that the writing is complete. The subject should arrive through an observation, a fact, an image or a lived detail rather than through a pitch.

The three public thematic directions are Reset, Renew and Becoming where those terms are used in the site taxonomy. The Inner Work public section is now presented as Unfolding. Taxonomy may help the site organise its work, but it must not become visible coaching language inside an essay unless it is genuinely part of the sentence and the user has approved it.

## Writing rules for new or reviewed pieces

The voice is first person, reflective and conversational. The opening observation is written in the present tense. UK spelling is used throughout. There are no dashes anywhere in the finished copy. There is no comma before “and”. These are mechanical checks as well as editorial preferences.

The prose remains continuous. Use plain paragraphs only. Do not add H2 subheadings, bullet points, numbered lists, FAQ blocks, key takeaway boxes or other modular content structures inside an essay. The piece should move from one observation into a wider idea without interrupting its reading surface.

A new piece normally has a minimum of 250 words, but an existing short piece is not to be padded or rewritten merely to meet that threshold. Length is subordinate to integrity. If twenty two words are all a piece needs, it may remain twenty two words.

Coaching strategy stays private. Do not name a coaching focus in public copy. Do not write “book a session”, “let’s explore your path”, “take the next step with me” or any other sentence that turns the essay into a pitch. The closing line should belong to the essay. It may be a statement, image, small instruction, question or unfinished thought. Vary the shape of the ending.

## The three step editorial method

First, anchor the piece in one real and checkable fact. The fact must be defensible before it appears on the page. A source should be recorded privately for the working file or editorial notes. Do not use a precise statistic merely because it sounds authoritative.

Second, name the private human focus separately from the public text. It may be self doubt, comparison, burnout, perfectionism or another concern. The focus helps select details and determine the emotional landing. It must not appear as a coaching label in the essay.

Third, let the closing line carry an unnamed invitation that arises from the subject itself. If the closing line could be mistaken for marketing copy, remove the sales pressure and return to the image, fact or thought that belongs to the piece.

## Section boundaries

| Section | Public attention | Editorial test |
|---|---|---|
| Ecology | The outer world, including animals, trees, soil, water, weather and waste | The human meaning arrives through a physical or living anchor. If the piece turns directly towards inner life without that anchor, it belongs elsewhere. |
| Inner Work or Unfolding | Inner life, attention, self talk, courage, change and identity | The piece can be personal, but it should avoid coaching terminology and maintain a specific lived or observed entry point. |
| Wellbeing | The body, rest, movement, health claims and the conditions of living | Health claims require especially careful sourcing. Avoid certainty where the evidence is limited. |
| Modern Tools | Technology, AI, digital habits and systems | The piece should examine what a tool changes in a human day rather than praise novelty. |
| Tea Notes | The threshold into a private conversation | It may be more direct and welcoming, but it should remain spacious and personal rather than sales led. |
| About | The person and the threads behind the work | The page should feel intimate and composed. Avoid oversized display copy that competes with the portrait. |

## Fact checking and source notes

For every new factual opening, record the source separately from the public article. Prefer a primary research paper, official institution, government publication, recognised scientific body or original dataset. Record the exact claim, the source URL, the publication date where available and any qualification required by the evidence.

Do not rely on a search result snippet. Do not turn an association into a cause. Do not present a study on a narrow group as a universal statement. Do not use a number when the source only supports a broad direction. If the fact cannot be checked, remove the fact or change the sentence so it makes no unsupported claim.

## Repetition control

Before publication, scan the entire set rather than only the new piece. Check repeated proper nouns, repeated openings, repeated metaphors, repeated sentence shapes, repeated factual anchors and repeated closing structures. A repeated word that is necessary to the subject is not automatically a problem. Repeated phrasing that makes separate pieces sound templated is a problem.

The same review should check for dashes, American spelling, comma before “and”, coaching language, unsupported numbers, headings, lists and meta descriptions over 155 characters. Existing copy should be reported when it fails a rule rather than silently rewritten.

## Image standards

Every image must be confirmed before the text is written around it. Do not invent a visual detail that is absent from the live photograph. Preserve the existing aspect ratio unless a deliberate crop has been approved.

Alt text should be useful to the person who cannot see the image. It should explain the image’s role in the page or piece rather than provide a keyword stuffed inventory of objects. Story images now use an article led form such as “Visual companion to ‘What Blue and Blaze Have Taught Me About Time’.” This avoids invented detail while connecting the image to the essay’s subject. Decorative images should use empty alt text rather than descriptive text that adds no information.

Image filenames should remain short and descriptive. The page should place an image near relevant text. The same image should use a stable URL across the site where possible. The current site keeps images eager loaded by requirement. To control the cost of that decision, maintain 480 pixel and 900 pixel responsive derivatives, use a correct `srcset` and `sizes` value and give only the first visible candidate high fetch priority.

## Typography and interface hierarchy

Cormorant Garamond is the literary layer. Use it for the Tea with Ree mark, page headings, article titles and quiet section headings. Manrope is the functional layer. Use it for body copy, navigation, metadata, labels, controls and forms.

Do not use uppercase for ordinary sentences or coaching calls to action. Uppercase with measured letter spacing is appropriate for small section labels and navigation where it provides orientation. It should not be used to make a sentence feel more important than its content.

Tea Notes defines the non home rhythm. Use a readable central measure, restrained horizontal rules, consistent 24 pixel mobile side margins and generous distance between image, copy and the next section. Do not recreate the earlier oversized About treatment. The homepage can carry more expressive scale because it is the entrance rather than the reading room.

## Quiet luxury interaction principles

The site should feel responsive without appearing animated for its own sake. Good interactions include a fine underline that draws under a text link, a small arrow movement, a slight image lift, an accent line that settles into place and a quiet focus outline. Avoid floating cards, glossy gradients, excessive parallax, large bouncing entrances and filled utility pills.

The article control is intentionally presented as “Pass it on ↗”. Its secondary actions are “Copy” and “Share”. Share should use the native share sheet when available and copy the URL as a fallback when it is not. Copy and Share must provide clear feedback without changing the article layout.

## SEO and crawler structure

Every indexable page should have one meaningful title, one H1, a concise meta description, a canonical URL, Open Graph title and description, an appropriate image and valid structured data where the page type supports it. Article pages should use Article or BlogPosting data only when the visible page supports those claims. Section hubs should use CollectionPage or an equivalent collection type.

The complete sitemap contains every published HTML page and excludes partial templates such as `nav.html` and `first-pour-block.html`. The robots file allows public HTML, CSS, JavaScript and image resources to remain crawlable and points to the sitemap. It also explicitly allows common AI crawlers. Robots rules are a traffic and crawling instruction, not a privacy mechanism. Do not put private material in a public repository and do not use robots.txt as a substitute for noindex or access control.

The site now includes `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt` and `ai.txt`. These files provide attribution guidance, canonical URLs and an expanded content index. They do not replace normal HTML, internal links, page metadata or the XML sitemap.

The current Google Search Console pathway is not connected in this session. The site files are ready for submission, but no external Google property was altered. After deployment, submit `https://teawithree.com/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console if Rehana has access to the verified property.

## Performance standards

The target is 95 or higher where the platform and loading requirements make it realistic. Lighthouse is a laboratory measurement and varies with network, server response and test timing. Report the score, LCP, CLS and the cause of any shortfall rather than changing the site to chase one run.

The first visible image should be discoverable early, have intrinsic dimensions and use a responsive source. The first visible card should not be hidden behind an entrance animation. Below fold images may remain eager loaded when required, but they should use low fetch priority and correctly sized derivatives. Do not reintroduce lazy loading without explicit approval.

The present limitation is that eager loading every story image creates unavoidable network competition on the long section hubs. Compression and responsive derivatives reduce the cost but cannot remove the cost altogether. This is why a page may have perfect SEO, accessibility and best practices while its performance score remains below 95.

## Publishing workflow

Before editing, make a clean Git commit or confirm the working tree state. Make the smallest change that solves the identified problem. Do not mix article rewrites with infrastructure work. Run `git diff --check` before committing. Validate JSON files with a parser. Validate XML well formedness. Check that every sitemap URL maps to a real page.

After publishing, test the raw GitHub branch and the public GitHub Pages URL separately. GitHub Pages can serve a previous deployment for a short period. When cache busting is needed, update the stylesheet query string rather than making speculative visual changes.

## Final pre publication checklist

| Check | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Copy scope | No article paragraphs changed during an infrastructure task. |
| Length | Existing short articles remain short unless a separate rewrite is approved. |
| Fact integrity | New factual claims have a private source note. |
| House style | UK spelling, no dashes, no comma before “and”, no marketing pitch. |
| Structure | Plain paragraphs only inside essays. |
| Images | Confirmed image, intrinsic dimensions, contextual alt text and eager loading. |
| Responsive images | 480 pixel and 900 pixel derivatives are used where available. |
| SEO | Title, description, canonical, H1, Open Graph and structured data are coherent. |
| Crawler files | Sitemap is complete and robots points to it. |
| AI index | `ai.txt`, `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` contain accurate canonical links. |
| Accessibility | Meaningful alt text, keyboard focus, contrast and usable tap targets. |
| Performance | Check LCP, CLS, image delivery, scripts and first card rendering. |
| Deployment | Raw branch, Pages URL and cache-busted assets agree. |
