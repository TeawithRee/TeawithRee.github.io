About

Kindness was the beginning. Growth is the path.

I believe change begins in the small moments: a story that shifts perspective, a conversation that opens a door, a cup of tea that slows the pace long enough to notice what matters.

I know what it feels like to leap before you are ready. Fear and courage often arrive together and it is in that space that growth happens. Travel has taught me this, not only through the places I have seen but through the people who challenged me to see differently. My love for animals, sustainability and freedom continues to remind me that connection is the real measure of discovery.

I am drawn to the future and the possibilities it holds. AI and new ways of thinking excite me because they expand how we learn and how we imagine what could be.

Healing practices, writing and reflection are part of how I make sense of the world. They are also how I help others make sense of theirs. Coaching is not about fixing people, it is about creating space for clarity, courage and the next step forward.

Tea with Ree is where these threads come together. It is a place for stories, wellbeing, friendships and the questions that make life interesting. If you are ready to explore your own next chapter, I am here to walk alongside you.
