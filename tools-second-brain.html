Building a Second Brain

July 1, 2026

Your mind is for having ideas, not holding them. That's the idea behind the second brain movement — and honestly, it's changed how I think. Externalise your thinking so that your biological brain is free to do what it does best: connect, create, and wonder.

The mind is for having ideas, not holding them. A second brain is not a crutch. It is a prosthetic for thought.

The CODE Method

Tiago Forte's CODE method structures the process:

  1. Capture. Capture everything that resonates. The fleeting thought. The interesting paragraph. The connection between two ideas.
  2. Organise. Organise it by project or area of interest. Make it searchable and taggable.
  3. Distill. Distill it into your own words. The act of rewriting is the act of understanding.
  4. Express. Express it through writing, speaking, or creating. Turn consumption into production.

The Payoff

After two years of building a second brain, I no longer feel the anxiety of forgetting. I know that what matters is saved. I know that I can find it when I need it. The system shapes the mind. The mind shapes the system. The loop is virtuous.
